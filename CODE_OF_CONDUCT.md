# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (Summary)

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in SOMEDAY a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender, experience level, nationality, or religion.

## Our Standards
- Be kind, respectful, and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers and patiently explain the static-SPA constraints in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
- No harassment, trolling, insulting comments, or unwelcome advances.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the user.

## Enforcement
Unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers via GitHub issues / contact on the repo at https://github.com/aruninf/someday. Maintainers may remove comments, close PRs/issues, or ban contributors who violate this code.

Full text: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
